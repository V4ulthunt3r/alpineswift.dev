Why Vista

The first images decide.

In the App Store, most people only look at the screenshots before they download or scroll on. Building them for several device sizes and languages, and redoing them for every update, quickly eats days in design tools without the right workflow.

Design once, export everywhere

One project holds every store format and language. Copy is shared; layout tweaks per format stay separate.

Copy that fits

Vista checks every language for clipped or overlapping text and can shrink oversized type for you, with a list of every change.

Fresh screens, no fresh start

Repeatable simulator captures bring in current screens in every language. Copy and layouts stay as they are.

Features

What Vista does.

Templates and looks

Nine templates from Feature Story to Panorama and 46 looks. Edit devices, gradients, your own backgrounds and zoom callouts right on the canvas, with undo for every step.

Localisation with preview

Every language side by side, with progress, filters for missing or overlong copy and a live preview. JSON exchange with translators and tools.

Export for the store and fastlane

PNG or JPEG for selected formats and languages, panoramas cleanly split into single images. Optionally in fastlane's folder structure.

Straight to App Store Connect

With your own API key: Vista checks dimensions against Apple's current specs and shows a preview before uploading. Nothing is submitted for App Review.

Short marketing videos

Your finished screens become a scene sequence with zoom, rise and fade, exported as MP4 in HD or Full HD.

On-device AI studio

On supported Macs with macOS 26, Apple's local model proposes story, copy and colour concept from your screenshots. No cloud AI provider; everything stays editable.

Shared by all four tools

Built like our apps.

Once you share a saved project with local assistants from the File menu, Vista opens a windowless local MCP connection. An assistant can read the screen copy, check it and suggest improvements. Nothing is applied until you accept the proposal in Vista. Export and upload stay with you.

  • Local first

    Your data lives on your devices. No Alpine Swift account and no server of ours in between.

  • Native to Apple

    Built with Swift and SwiftUI, with light and dark mode and the interaction patterns you know from Apple.

  • Assistants welcome

    Every tool ships a local MCP interface. AI assistants may read, check and suggest.

  • You decide

    Nothing is applied, published or submitted unless you confirmed it yourself. No silent automation.
