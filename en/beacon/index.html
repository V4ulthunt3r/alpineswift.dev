Beacon

Your App Store presence in one place.

Beacon is a workspace for everything people see in the App Store: copy, keywords, screenshots, versions and reviews. Native on the Mac, plus iPhone and iPad, connected to App Store Connect through your own access.

Why Beacon

App Store Connect is a maze of forms.

Several languages, several device sizes, regular updates: keeping a store listing current means clicking through dozens of forms, and it is easy to lose track of what changed when and whether it made any difference.

Everything in one place

Metadata, screenshots, releases and reviews in one app instead of spread across many pages.

Draft first, then live

Changes start as local drafts. Only what you reviewed and confirmed one by one goes to Apple.

Learn, don't guess

An ASO journal records what you changed and why, and compares downloads before and after.

Features

What Beacon does.

Metadata and languages

Store copy and keywords for every language side by side, with character and byte limits checked before anything goes to Apple.

Screenshot Studio

Import screenshots, arrange them per language and device and check them against Apple's current specs before uploading.

Release Center

One workspace per version: assign a build, write release notes, check required fields and submit for App Review. Every step confirmed on its own.

Reviews and replies

Filter reviews by stars, territory and unanswered, prepare replies as drafts and publish them deliberately.

ASO analytics

Downloads by territory, source and device over 7, 28 and 90 days. Plus actions with hypothesis, goal and a before/after comparison.

Optional AI assistant

On-device with Apple Intelligence or with your own OpenAI key. Suggestions cite their sources and are only applied after your review.

Shared by all three tools

Built like our apps.

Through its local MCP server, an assistant can read explicitly shared metadata, analyse keywords and prepare changes. It cannot publish, submit or read credentials. Every proposal needs your acceptance in Beacon.

  • Local first

    Your data lives on your devices. No Alpine Swift account and no server of ours in between.

  • Native to Apple

    Built with Swift and SwiftUI, with light and dark mode and the interaction patterns you know from Apple.

  • Assistants welcome

    Every tool ships a local MCP interface. AI assistants may read, check and suggest.

  • You decide

    Nothing is applied, published or submitted unless you confirmed it yourself. No silent automation.
