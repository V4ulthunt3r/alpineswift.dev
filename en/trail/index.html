Trail

The route for your app projects.

Trail is project management for indie developers: tasks, milestones, notes and files in one place, native on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Your data stays on your devices and syncs through your own iCloud.

Why Trail

Built for projects with one person on board.

Big project tools are made for teams with roles, sprints and seat licences. If you build apps on your own or as a pair, you need something else: quick to capture, available offline and close to the code.

Quick to capture

Inbox, Today and My Tasks: ideas land in the right project straight away and come back when it is their turn.

Close to the code

Issues, branches, pull requests and commits from GitHub sit right on the task they belong to.

No third-party server

Sync runs through your private iCloud. There is no Alpine Swift account and no project data on our machines.

Features

What Trail does.

List or board

Every project as a list or a Kanban board with fully configurable status columns. Move and reorder tasks with drag and drop.

Milestones and dependencies

Subtasks, checklists, tags, milestones and dependencies that never allow a cycle. Plus estimated and actual effort.

Timeline and metrics

Planning metrics and a timeline show whether the project is on track before the deadline starts to bite.

Versioned notes

Concepts and decisions as versioned notes right inside the project. Earlier versions stay traceable.

Files where they belong

Drafts, screenshots and documents belong to the project, a task or a note. With type filters and Quick Look; originals are stored with a checksum.

One search for everything

Projects and tasks in a single search, filtered by favourites, status, priority and due date.

Shared by all three tools

Built like our apps.

On the Mac, Trail ships the local adapter trail-mcp. Once you share a project, an AI assistant can read it, check titles and dates and suggest improvements. Nothing is applied until you approve it in the review. Sharing ends as soon as you revoke it or quit Trail.

  • Local first

    Your data lives on your devices. No Alpine Swift account and no server of ours in between.

  • Native to Apple

    Built with Swift and SwiftUI, with light and dark mode and the interaction patterns you know from Apple.

  • Assistants welcome

    Every tool ships a local MCP interface. AI assistants may read, check and suggest.

  • You decide

    Nothing is applied, published or submitted unless you confirmed it yourself. No silent automation.
